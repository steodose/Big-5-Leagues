Big 5 Leagues

Live standings for Europe's five biggest leagues — the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, and Ligue 1.

Rest-of-season forecast. Sortable — click a header.

For each of the Big 5 leagues, the Between the Pipes model plays out the rest of the season 10,000 times and counts how often each club wins the title, makes the Champions League, qualifies for Europe, or goes down.

Tables

Standings, records, and goals are pulled live from ESPN. Points are the global standard (3 for a win, 1 for a draw). Ordering follows each league's own official tiebreakers. Form shows each club's last six league results, oldest to newest — blue for a win, grey for a draw, red for a loss (hover a pill for the opponent and score).

Projections

The Projections tab projects the rest of the season. Each club's attacking and defensive strength is estimated from its goals scored and conceded — regressed toward the league average so a small sample or a hot finishing run doesn't dominate — and turned into an expected scoreline for every remaining fixture, with a home-field edge. Those fixtures are played out with random Poisson goals thousands of times, starting from the table as it stands today. The probabilities shown — winning the title, a top-four or European finish, or going down — are simply how often each happened across all of those simulated seasons. Switch to Positions for each club's chance of finishing in every place in the table, or Outcomes for the same odds as stacked bars split by title, Champions League, Europe, mid-table, and relegation.
