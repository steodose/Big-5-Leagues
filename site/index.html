A live look at the league tables across Europe's Big 5 — one clean, sortable table per competition, with the European and relegation places marked exactly as each league draws them.

The tables

Standings, records, and goals are pulled live from ESPN. Points are the global standard (3 for a win, 1 for a draw). Ordering follows each league's own official tiebreakers — ESPN already applies them (and any points deductions), so the order you see is the order that counts. PPG is points per game, handy mid-season when clubs have played an uneven number of matches. The Form guide shows each club's last six league results, oldest to newest — blue for a win, grey for a draw, red for a loss (hover a pill for the opponent and score).

The colored bands

The colored edge beside a club's position marks the zone it currently sits in — Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, or relegation. Exactly which places qualify differs by country and shifts season to season (European coefficients, cup winners, and the like), so the bands are read straight from the league table rather than hard-coded.

Switching leagues

Use the dropdown to jump between the five leagues; the table, the zone legend, and the crest all follow. Every column is sortable — click a header — and each table exports to PNG or CSV.

The forecast

The Simulations tab projects the rest of the season. Each club's attacking and defensive strength is estimated from its goals scored and conceded — regressed toward the league average so a small sample or a hot finishing run doesn't dominate — and turned into an expected scoreline for every remaining fixture, with a home-field edge. Those fixtures are played out with random Poisson goals thousands of times, starting from the table as it stands today. The probabilities shown — winning the title, a top-four or European finish, or going down — are simply how often each happened across all of those simulated seasons.
